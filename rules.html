Rules — TopRankSM

How the public leaderboard works. Simple, public and the same for everyone.

How it works

  • Pick a board — there are 11 boards: TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, Snapchat, LinkedIn, Threads, Websites, Apps and the Fan Board.
  • Place your claim — enter your profile handle, link, country and city, and your claim amount.
  • Highest claim takes #1 — your claim is your rank. Money = Rank.

Claiming rules

  • First claim on any board: $1 (about Rs 280).
  • Minimum raise: $1 above the current top claim.
  • Restricted listings: adult, gambling, weapons and scam/Ponzi websites or apps cannot be claimed — the claim form blocks them automatically.
  • Tie rule: if two claims are equal, the earlier claim keeps the higher rank.
  • Every claim is public — anyone can see every challenger, amount and rank, including the public click counter on each listing.
  • Claims must be for a real, public profile, page, website or app. Fake or abusive entries can be removed by the admin.

Payments

  • Card payment (via Whop): your claim goes live automatically as soon as the payment succeeds.
  • Manual payment (Easypaisa, Nayapay, Sadapay, Upaisa, Faysal Bank or USDT TRC20 — details on the home page): your claim goes live after the admin verifies and approves it.
  • All claims are final and non-refundable — see the Refund Policy.

Seasons & the Hall of Fame

  • Boards run in Seasons of 3 months. Season 1 ends on 18 January 2027.
  • When a Season ends, the boards reset and the top 10 of every board are saved forever in the Hall of Fame.
