FAQ — TopRankSM

Quick answers about the public leaderboard.

How do I get #1?

Claim higher than the current #1 on that board — at least $1 more than their claim.

Is my payment refundable?

No. All claims are final and non-refundable. Claim carefully.

Can I claim on someone else's ID?

Yes. Anyone can claim on any real ID — your own or someone else's.

What happens when the Season ends?

Boards reset to $1, champions enter the Hall of Fame, and a new Season begins.

How do I pay?

Easypaisa, Nayapay, Sadapay, Upaisa or bank transfer. Send the amount, enter your Transaction ID (TID); your claim goes live after verification.

What is the Fan Board?

One cross-platform board where fans claim for their favorite creators from any social media platform.

What is the difference between All Time and Today?

All Time shows the full Season ranking; Today shows only claims placed today.
